How Result Sync Works

Automatic subjects import from Result Management

Purpose & Objective

Eliminates manual re-entry of subjects. It automatically imports official subject lists configured in Result Management directly into the Academic Plan for your duty classes.

Data Source

Subjects are retrieved from each class's examination configurations (resultSchemas) for the selected academic year.

2-Step Selective Process
  1. Select Classes: Choose which of your assigned classes you want to synchronize.
  2. Review & Filter: Preview discovered subjects for each class, uncheck any unwanted items, and import only what you need.
